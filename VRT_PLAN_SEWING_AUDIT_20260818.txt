4) ROOT CAUSES FIXED
- Weekly KPI used the MAX manpower and MAX efficiency of each line across the week, then averaged those maxima.
- Daily/weekly calculations excluded staffed zero-output lines, shrinking the denominator and overstating efficiency.
- Weekly parser rounded efficiency and discarded zero-output staffed lines.
- Weekly parser did not retain Produced Minutes / Attended Minutes.
- Production Plan parser allowed footer/grand-total rows to inherit the preceding line number.

5) NEW RULES
- Efficiency first choice: exact Produced Standard Minutes / Attended Minutes.
- If exact minute fields are unavailable: reported efficiency weighted by manpower × 480.
- Staffed zero-output lines remain in denominator.
- Daily detail quantity > Weekly summary quantity > Target/DPO quantity, preventing duplicate actual pieces.
- Production Plan formula/footer rows never inherit a line.
- August 2026 onward Line 12 is excluded.
